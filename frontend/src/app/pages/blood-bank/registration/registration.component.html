How to find your Latitude & Longitude on Google Maps

Find the exact location of your blood bank on Google Maps:

  1. Open Google Maps (maps.google.com) on your computer or phone.
  2. Search or right-click on your blood bank's exact facility location on the map.
  3. Select the coordinates displayed at the top of the context menu (for example: 19.0760, 72.8777).
  4. Paste the first number as Latitude and the second number as Longitude.
