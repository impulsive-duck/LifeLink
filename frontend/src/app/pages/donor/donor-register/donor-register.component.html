🔬 Hemoglobin Donation Safety Standard

National health guidelines require voluntary blood donors to maintain a minimum hemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL. This ensures that donating 350ml–450ml of blood will not cause fatigue, anemia, or dizziness, and that the recipient receives healthy red blood cells capable of optimal oxygen transport.

💡 Tip: Iron-rich foods (spinach, dates, legumes, beetroot) help maintain healthy hemoglobin levels.
